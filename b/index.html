01 · 背景与问题01 · Background & problem

电容式传感器为什么会漂移Why capacitive sensors drift

电容式土壤湿度传感器通过测量介电常数推算含水量，但这个换算关系并不稳定：温度变化会改变探头周围介质的介电特性，土壤盐分波动会影响电场分布，电极表面氧化和积垢会逐渐降低灵敏度。三者叠加后，同一支传感器在部署第一周和第三个月给出的读数可能相差数个百分点，而且偏移方向因批次、埋深和灌溉方式而异。Capacitive soil-moisture sensors infer water content from a dielectric-constant measurement, but that mapping is not stable: temperature shifts the dielectric properties of the surrounding medium, salinity fluctuations distort the electric field, and electrode oxidation or fouling gradually reduces sensitivity. Stacked together, the same sensor can report readings several percentage points apart between its first week and its third month in the field, with the direction of drift varying by batch, burial depth, and irrigation pattern.

大多数十美元级别的模块没有片上温度补偿，出厂标定只对应实验室条件下的单一介质。把这类传感器直接用于长期无人值守的田间监测，读数会随时间悄悄失真，而且这种失真不会触发任何报警——它只是让灌溉决策逐渐建立在错误的数据上。Most sub-$10 modules ship with no on-chip temperature compensation, and the factory calibration is valid for a single lab-condition medium only. Left running unattended in the field, readings quietly drift out of true — and nothing about that failure mode trips an alarm; it just lets irrigation decisions accumulate on top of bad data.

02 · 协议设计02 · Protocol design

配对参考探头与周期性微校准A paired reference probe and periodic micro-calibration

配对参考探头Paired reference probe

每组部署传感器搭配一支密封参考探头，探头内部介质固定、不受土壤盐分影响，用作漂移基线的锚点。Each deployment group pairs with a sealed reference probe whose internal medium is fixed and unaffected by soil salinity — it anchors the drift baseline.

周期性微校准脉冲Periodic micro-calibration pulse

每 6 小时施加一次低幅度激励脉冲，把响应曲线与出厂基线比对，得到当前时刻的漂移估计。A low-amplitude excitation pulse fires every 6 hours; comparing the response curve against the factory baseline yields a present-moment drift estimate.

分段温度补偿Piecewise temperature compensation

按 <10°C、10–30°C、>30°C 三段分别拟合补偿曲线，避免单一线性模型在极端温度下失真。Compensation curves are fitted separately for <10°C, 10–30°C, and >30°C bands, avoiding the breakdown a single linear model shows at temperature extremes.

低功耗设计Low-power budget

校准脉冲本身耗时不到 200 毫秒，按 6 小时间隔运行，额外功耗在整机预算中可以忽略。The pulse itself runs under 200 ms; at a 6-hour cadence, the added power draw is negligible against the device's overall budget.

03 · 校准流程03 · Calibration procedure

从部署到持续补偿From deployment to ongoing compensation

  1. 部署时记录出厂响应曲线与参考探头基线；record the factory response curve and reference-probe baseline at deployment;
  2. 每 6 小时触发一次微校准脉冲并采集响应；trigger a micro-calibration pulse and collect the response every 6 hours;
  3. 按温度分区比对响应曲线，计算漂移估计值；compare response curves within the matching temperature band to compute the drift estimate;
  4. 若参考探头或脉冲响应超出正常范围，本次估计标记为低置信度；flag the estimate low-confidence if the reference probe or pulse response falls outside its normal range;
  5. 将补偿值写入读数流水线，供上层灌溉逻辑直接使用。write the compensation value into the reading pipeline for direct use by the irrigation logic above it.

05 · 适用场景与边界05 · Where it fits, and its limits

适合谁用，暂时不适合谁Who it suits, and who it doesn't yet

这套协议对大田和温室的长期无人值守监测收益最明显——部署周期越长、人工巡检越少，漂移带来的隐性成本越高。当前验证覆盖的盐分范围对应常规灌溉土壤；在高盐碱地或海水入侵区域，参考探头的锚定假设会失效，需要另外处理。传感器使用超过两年后的电极老化补偿也还未纳入验证范围。The protocol pays off most on long-running, low-touch field or greenhouse deployments — the less frequent the manual inspection, the more drift's hidden cost adds up. The salinity range validated so far corresponds to ordinary irrigated soil; in high-salinity or seawater-intrusion sites, the reference-probe anchoring assumption breaks down and needs separate handling. Electrode aging beyond two years of continuous use also falls outside what's been validated.

06 · 常见问题06 · FAQ

FAQFAQ

需要额外硬件吗？Does it need extra hardware?

每组部署需要一支密封参考探头和支持可编程激励脉冲的驱动电路，多数现有模块可通过固件升级支持后者。Each deployment group needs one sealed reference probe and a driver circuit capable of a programmable excitation pulse; most existing modules can add the latter via a firmware update.

校准间隔可以调整吗？Can the calibration interval be changed?

可以，6 小时是当前部署使用的默认值；缩短间隔能进一步压低漂移，但会线性增加功耗。Yes — 6 hours is the default used in the current deployment; shortening it lowers drift further but increases power draw linearly.

适用于所有传感器型号吗？Does it work across all sensor models?

目前的补偿模型是针对测试所用批次拟合的，跨型号和跨厂商部署需要重新采集响应曲线。The current compensation model is fitted to the batch used in this deployment; moving across models or vendors requires re-collecting the response curves.

和温度查表补偿相比优势在哪？What's the advantage over a temperature look-up table?

查表法只补偿温度这一个变量，对电极老化和盐分漂移无能为力；PetrichorSync 用参考探头持续标定当前状态，能同时吸收这几类漂移来源。A look-up table only compensates for temperature and does nothing for electrode aging or salinity-driven drift; PetrichorSync's reference probe continuously calibrates against present conditions, absorbing all three drift sources at once.
